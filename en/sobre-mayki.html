02 / School journey

The path so far

I studied at Escola Ecológica through fifth grade. From sixth through ninth grade, I attended Colégio João Cândido. I am now continuing my education at the Federal Institute of Paraná (IFPR) in the Information Technology technical program.

03 / Date in history

04 / Today

Studies, sports, and connection

I currently study at IFPR in the Information Technology program. Sports and video games remain among my interests, as do spending time with friends and family. I have advanced to the second stage of OBMEP and was runner-up in the Paraná state judo championship.
