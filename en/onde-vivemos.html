02 / Places

Where we live

The places featured in the group's stories: Nova Santa Rosa and Toledo, in Paraná, Brazil.

Nova Santa Rosa · Paraná

Bruno and Isaque

Town

A town connected to agriculture

In the group's accounts, Nova Santa Rosa is described as a small, well-organized town shaped by agriculture and livestock. German and Italian settlement, and its connection to the city of Santa Rosa in Rio Grande do Sul, are mentioned as references for the town's history and name. The Municipal Lake is cited as a local leisure space.

Neighborhood

Downtown

Bruno identifies downtown as his neighborhood. He highlights its street layout, cultural heritage, and historical landmarks. Isaque also says he lives in Nova Santa Rosa, but the original material does not identify his specific neighborhood.

Toledo · Paraná

Mayki

Town

Paraná's agribusiness capital

Mayki describes Toledo as an important regional center, known for agribusiness, quality of life, sports traditions, and educational opportunities in western Paraná.

Neighborhood

Vila Becker

Mayki's account says he grew up in Vila Becker, a neighborhood associated with the pioneering Becker family. The presence of the Unioeste campus and the mix of university life with quiet residential streets are among the details in the original text.
